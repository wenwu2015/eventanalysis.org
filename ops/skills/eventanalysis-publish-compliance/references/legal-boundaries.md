# Legal-pack boundaries

Legal packs are private, versioned inputs signed by local counsel. An agent may detect official-source changes and prepare a draft comparison, but it cannot activate a pack or create the signature hash.

Validation covers defamation and insult, national and ethnic attack, privacy, minors, image rights, copyright, trademark, data protection, media regulation, injury and medical claims, betting, match fixing, doping, crime, corruption, discipline, disclosure duties, and complaint deadlines.

The target market list and content nexus are different checks. A locale's market is not inherited by every country that uses the language. A content item also needs valid coverage for the event venue, participating countries, player and team affiliations, competition organizer, and publisher jurisdiction.

When law or facts are uncertain, choose BLOCK. Legal uncertainty is never eligible for automated rewriting and retry.
